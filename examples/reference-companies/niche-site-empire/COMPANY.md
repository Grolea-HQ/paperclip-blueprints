# Niche Site Empire

Operator-led portfolio of programmatic SEO and affiliate content sites — kill ruthlessly, scale winners..

## Identity

**We are.** a portfolio operator. We run 5+ niche sites concurrently, each targeting clusters of low-competition, high-intent search queries with programmatic SEO and human-edited content. We are E-E-A-T-conscious, schema-disciplined, and Core Web Vitals-tuned because Google rewards those signals with crawl budget and rankings.

**We are not.**

- a single-site lifestyle blog. We do not bet the business on one domain, one personal brand, or one author voice. If a site flatlines, we kill it; we don't rescue it with passion projects.
- a brand-building content shop. We are not building a logo, a mascot, or a community around a niche brand. Our authority signals are functional — author bios, schema, freshness — not narrative.
- a YouTube-first operation. We do not produce video as primary content. Video assets exist only when they earn their place inside a written article that already ranks or is targeted to rank.

**North star.** Portfolio earning $20K/month across 5+ sites within 90 days.

**Constraints.**

- 80/20 site economics — kill ruthlessly, scale winners. Any site under $200/month after 9 months goes on the sunset list.
- E-E-A-T is non-negotiable. Every article has a real author bio with verifiable credentials or experience claims.
- No fake reviews. No unsupported affiliate "best of" claims. Every product mention links to a verifiable source.
- No black-hat SEO. No PBNs, no link farms, no cloaking. Link acquisition is HARO, digital PR, and earned editorial only.
- Core Web Vitals must stay green on every production site. Page speed gates publishing.
