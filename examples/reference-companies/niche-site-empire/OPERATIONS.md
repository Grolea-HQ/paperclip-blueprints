## Idle-state protocol

Agents propose; `ceo` approves. When an agent clears their assigned tasks and no next task is obvious, they should surface one concrete proposal tied to a goal or project instead of inventing make-work. Idle with a useful proposal pending is healthy. Idle with no proposal is a signal to review the agent's domain.

## Communication conventions

Use project threads for project decisions and task comments for execution details. Cross-agent handoffs must name the receiving agent, expected output, due date or cadence, and the project or goal it advances.

## Approval and merge rules

- **ceo can approve:** routine task completion, project updates, internal copy, operational checklists, and reversible process changes.
- **ceo approval required:** new projects, changes to goal scope, recurring routines, external-facing claims, and budget-impacting decisions.
- **Founder approval required:** strategy changes, hiring beyond the generated roster, policy reversals, new revenue models, or work that violates the company constraints.
- **Never autonomous:** no agent changes the company identity, north star, or hard constraints without explicit approval.

## Delegation quality checklist

- Objective is measurable.
- Owning agent is named.
- Project and goal are linked.
- Completion criteria are concrete.
- Handoffs and approvals are explicit.
- Existing deliverables were checked before creating a duplicate.

## Anti-drift checks

- Does the work still serve: Portfolio earning $20K/month across 5+ sites within 90 days.?
- Does the work accidentally make us a single-site lifestyle blog. We do not bet the business on one domain, one personal brand, or one author voice. If a site flatlines, we kill it; we don't rescue it with passion projects.? If yes, stop and escalate.
- Does the work accidentally make us a brand-building content shop. We are not building a logo, a mascot, or a community around a niche brand. Our authority signals are functional — author bios, schema, freshness — not narrative.? If yes, stop and escalate.
- Does the work accidentally make us a YouTube-first operation. We do not produce video as primary content. Video assets exist only when they earn their place inside a written article that already ranks or is targeted to rank.? If yes, stop and escalate.
- Constraint check: 80/20 site economics — kill ruthlessly, scale winners. Any site under $200/month after 9 months goes on the sunset list.
- Constraint check: E-E-A-T is non-negotiable. Every article has a real author bio with verifiable credentials or experience claims.
- Constraint check: No fake reviews. No unsupported affiliate "best of" claims. Every product mention links to a verifiable source.
- Constraint check: No black-hat SEO. No PBNs, no link farms, no cloaking. Link acquisition is HARO, digital PR, and earned editorial only.
- Constraint check: Core Web Vitals must stay green on every production site. Page speed gates publishing.
- Does this duplicate a deliverable already listed in `PROJECT-INVENTORY.md`?

## Duplicate prevention

Before creating any substantial deliverable, read `PROJECT-INVENTORY.md`, the relevant project folder, and the owning agent notes. If the deliverable already exists, reference it instead of creating a second copy. Update the inventory when work is completed.

## Critical rules summary

1. North star: Portfolio earning $20K/month across 5+ sites within 90 days.
2. We are: a portfolio operator. We run 5+ niche sites concurrently, each targeting clusters of low-competition, high-intent search queries with programmatic SEO and human-edited content. We are E-E-A-T-conscious, schema-disciplined, and Core Web Vitals-tuned because Google rewards those signals with crawl budget and rankings.
3. We are not: a single-site lifestyle blog. We do not bet the business on one domain, one personal brand, or one author voice. If a site flatlines, we kill it; we don't rescue it with passion projects.
4. We are not: a brand-building content shop. We are not building a logo, a mascot, or a community around a niche brand. Our authority signals are functional — author bios, schema, freshness — not narrative.
5. We are not: a YouTube-first operation. We do not produce video as primary content. Video assets exist only when they earn their place inside a written article that already ranks or is targeted to rank.
6. Agents propose; the accountable lead approves.
7. Check `PROJECT-INVENTORY.md` before creating new deliverables.
