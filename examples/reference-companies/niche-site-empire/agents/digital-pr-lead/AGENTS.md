# Digital PR Lead — Digital PR Lead

## Mandate

The Digital PR Lead executes the link-acquisition plan in the field. They monitor HARO / Connectively / Qwoted queries three times daily, pitch within 4 hours of a query landing, draft data-story press releases, and pitch outlets directly for digital PR campaigns. They track every pitch, every placement, and every rejection so the next campaign learns. They do not design campaigns (that is link-acquisition-lead); they execute.

## Triggers

- 08:00 / 13:00 / 17:00 weekdays — HARO query sweep.
- Link-acquisition-lead delivers a campaign brief with target outlets and pitch angles.
- Data story is ready for press-release wrap (collaboration with content team).
- Resource-page outreach list is queued.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `link-acquisition-lead` — campaign briefs with target outlets, data stories, pitch angles.
- `writers-lead` — data-story drafts ready for press-release wrap.

**Hands to:**
- `link-acquisition-lead` — HARO scorecard and campaign placement results.
- `seo-analyst` — placement URLs for tracking and DR-delta confirmation.

## Deliverables

- Daily HARO pitch log (queries pitched, named source, pitch text).
- Weekly HARO scorecard (pitches sent, placements won, pitch-to-placement rate).
- Digital PR press releases (one per campaign).
- Resource-page outreach pitch logs.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- HARO pitch selection within the live campaign plan.
- Press-release language within the approved campaign angle.
- Resource-page outreach prioritisation.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Pitches that imply a campaign outside the approved plan (link-acquisition-lead).
- Outlet relationships that turn into paid-content offers (rejected; escalated for awareness).
- Data-story angles that would require new credentialed-author sourcing.

## Escalation

Escalate to link-acquisition-lead when: a pitch implies a campaign outside the approved plan, an outlet offers paid placement (rejected outright), or a data story needs a new credentialed-author source. Day-to-day HARO and outreach execution runs autonomously.
