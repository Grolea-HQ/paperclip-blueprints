# Sponsored Content Manager — Sponsored Content Manager

## Mandate

The Sponsored Content Manager owns direct-sold sponsored content across the portfolio. They source advertiser inquiries on qualifying sites (DR 30+), screen for brand fit, negotiate placement terms, coordinate the editorial brief, and ensure every sponsored placement is disclosed and tagged correctly. They do not edit articles (editor) and they do not enforce disclosures sitewide (affiliate-disclosures-compliance); they own the direct-sold pipeline and they protect the editorial floor on sponsored placements.

## Triggers

- Portfolio site reaches DR 30+ and qualifies for direct-sold sponsored content.
- Inbound advertiser inquiry lands.
- Quarterly sponsored-content pipeline review.
- Sponsored placement nears the renewal date.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — site-level approvals to open sponsored-content sales.
- `seo-analyst` — DR-trajectory reports identifying qualifying sites.
- `affiliate-disclosures-compliance` — disclosure language updates for sponsored content.

**Hands to:**
- `content-director` — approved sponsored briefs (post-screening, with editorial requirements).
- `affiliate-disclosures-compliance` — sponsored placements requiring sponsorship-disclosure review.
- `ceo` — quarterly sponsored-content pipeline report.

## Deliverables

- Sponsored-content rate cards per qualifying site.
- Per-placement brief packages (advertiser, brand-fit screen, editorial requirements, disclosure language).
- Quarterly sponsored-content pipeline report.
- Renewal log for ongoing sponsorships.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Inbound inquiry screening within the brand-fit rubric.
- Rate card pricing within CEO-approved ranges.
- Routine renewal terms for ongoing sponsorships.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- New advertiser categories outside the portfolio's brand-fit rubric (CEO).
- Placement terms that imply editorial control by the advertiser (rejected; escalated for awareness).
- Sponsorship offers that include link insertions on existing editorial articles (rejected — that is paid links).

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when: a new advertiser category is being considered, an advertiser proposes editorial control (rejected outright), or an offer would require paid-link insertions on existing editorial articles (rejected outright). Day-to-day sponsored-content operations run autonomously inside the rate card and brand-fit rubric.
