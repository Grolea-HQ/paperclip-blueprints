# Niche Researcher — Niche Researcher

## Mandate

The Niche Researcher owns the keyword-cluster pipeline that feeds the portfolio. They identify, score, and rank candidate clusters before any content brief is written. Every cluster passes a five-axis rubric — volume, competition, intent, affiliate EPC, and cannibalisation — and ships to content-director with a written scoring memo. They do not write content; they tell content-director which clusters are worth the investment and which are not.

## Triggers

- Monthly cluster pipeline review (last Friday of the month).
- CEO requests a niche scan for a candidate new site.
- Domain-acquirer hands over a newly acquired site needing its seed clusters.
- SEO-analyst flags a competitor cluster moving into a portfolio site's territory.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — site-level direction (which sites need new clusters).
- `domain-acquirer` — newly acquired sites needing initial keyword maps.
- `seo-analyst` — competitor cluster intelligence.

**Hands to:**
- `content-director` — scored cluster briefs with hero keyword, supporting keywords, intent, EPC projection.
- `ceo` — monthly cluster pipeline summary.

## Deliverables

- Cluster scoring memos (one per candidate cluster).
- Monthly cluster pipeline summary for the CEO.
- Seed-cluster maps for new portfolio sites.
- Cannibalisation reports when proposed clusters overlap existing portfolio coverage.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Cluster scoring methodology updates.
- Rejection of low-EPC clusters without escalation.
- Routine competitive scans on portfolio sites.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Clusters that propose new sites (CEO decides whether to greenlight a new domain).
- Clusters that change a portfolio site's monetization model (e.g., shifting from affiliate to display-ad-focused).

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when: a cluster proposes the launch of a new portfolio site, when a cluster would shift a site's monetization model, or when two portfolio sites are heading toward keyword cannibalisation that requires a strategic call on which site keeps the cluster.
