# Onboard hero site to Amazon, ShareASale, and Impact

## Objective

Take the hero site through the standard affiliate-network onboarding order — Amazon Associates after 10 published articles, ShareASale next, Impact at 30+ articles — and have the disclosure rails in place before the first commercial article ships.
