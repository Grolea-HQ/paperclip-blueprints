# Draft founder voice guide v1

## Objective

Capture the founder's voice from the first four shipped issues into `editorial/voice-guide.md` so every agent on the masthead can apply it from issue 5 onward.

## Completion criteria

- One-page voice guide written following the `newsletter-voice-capture` skill protocol.
- Three sections: what the voice sounds like, what it never sounds like, ten phrases never to use.
- Drafted from 5 founder samples (or fewer if 5 issues have not yet shipped — supplement with podcast transcripts or long-form social).
- Reviewed and signed off by the CEO.
- Stored at `editorial/voice-guide.md` with a date stamp and a one-line rationale.
