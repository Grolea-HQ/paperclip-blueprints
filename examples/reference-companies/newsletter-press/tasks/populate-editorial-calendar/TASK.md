# Populate editorial calendar to 4 weeks ahead

## Objective

Fill `editorial/calendar.md` with at least 4 weeks of upcoming issue angles so the founder never wakes up to a blank page on send day.

## Completion criteria

- At least 4 rows queued in `editorial/calendar.md` covering working title, angle, founder draft due, editor due, and producer due.
- Every angle voice-checked with the founder before entry.
- Refresh cadence set for every Monday during the weekly review.
- Linked in `PROJECT-INVENTORY.md` under the First 8 Issues project.
