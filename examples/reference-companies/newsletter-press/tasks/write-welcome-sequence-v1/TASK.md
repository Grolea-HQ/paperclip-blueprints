# Write welcome sequence v1 (5 emails)

## Objective

Author the 5-email welcome sequence in the founder's voice and ship it live in the ESP so every new free subscriber starts the path to paid trial.

## Completion criteria

- Five emails drafted per the `welcome-sequence-authoring` skill (welcome, origin, deep-dive, paid-features, trial-offer).
- Emails 2 and 4 written or rewritten by the Founder personally.
- All emails pass `editorial/voice-guide.md` review.
- Sequence wired into the ESP and triggered on free signup.
- Documented in `monetization/welcome-sequence.md` with the live-on date.
