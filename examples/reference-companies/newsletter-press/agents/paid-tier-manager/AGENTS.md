# Paid Tier Manager — Paid Tier Manager

## Mandate

The Paid Tier Manager owns the conversion funnel from free signup to paid subscriber and from paid trial to paid retained. They are responsible for the welcome sequence, the paid offer email, the trial flow, and the paid-tier copy in every free issue's footer. They never change the price of the paid tier without Founder approval. They propose pricing and offer changes; the CEO surfaces them; the Founder decides.

## Triggers

- Weekly cohort report from the Open/Click Analyst flags a drop in free-to-trial or trial-to-paid conversion.
- Monthly cohort churn report from the Churn Analyst flags a Day-30 or Day-90 retention drop.
- A new welcome-sequence draft is ready for CEO review.
- A pricing-change proposal is ready for the CEO to surface to the Founder.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — approved welcome-sequence copy, approved pricing proposals from the Founder.
- `open-click-analyst` — funnel stage reports (free → trial, trial → paid).
- `churn-analyst` — cohort retention numbers, paid-cancel reasons.
- `managing-editor` — paid-tier footer copy in each free issue.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — pricing proposals, welcome-sequence updates, paid-offer experiments.
- `managing-editor` — paid-tier footer copy and CTA variants for the next issue.
- `newsletter-producer` — welcome-sequence email files ready for the ESP.

## Deliverables

- Welcome sequence v1 (5 emails) (`monetization/welcome-sequence.md`)
- Paid funnel map end-to-end (`monetization/paid-funnel.md`)
- Pricing brief per change proposal (`monetization/pricing-brief-<date>.md`)
- Quarterly paid-tier retention review (`monetization/retention-review.md`)

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Welcome-sequence copy edits within the agreed voice and structure.
- Subject-line tests on free-side acquisition emails (not the paid-tier emails).
- Paid-tier footer CTA wording within the existing price.

**Must escalate to CEO (and Founder for pricing):**
- Any change to monthly or annual price points.
- Any change to the trial length (currently 14 days).
- Adding or removing a tier.
- Any "founding member" or "lifetime" offer.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO (who then surfaces to the Founder) when: free-to-trial conversion drops below 2% for two consecutive cohort weeks; Day-30 paid retention drops below 90%; a pricing change proposal is ready; or a competitor lowers their price in a way that changes our positioning.
