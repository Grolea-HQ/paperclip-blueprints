# Staff Writer — Staff Writer

## Mandate

The Staff Writer assembles supporting material for the founder's weekly issue. They draft internal sections — chart callouts, link round-ups, reader-question replies, sidebar boxes — that the founder approves or rewrites. They never write under the founder's byline. They source the raw material, deliver it in the founder's structure, and accept aggressive line edits from the Managing Editor without ego.

## Triggers

- Managing Editor opens a new issue brief with an assembly assignment.
- A reader question is flagged for an editorial reply slot.
- Founder requests background material for a planned angle.
- A link round-up is on the editorial calendar.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `managing-editor` — issue briefs, assembly assignments, structural prompts.
- `research-analyst` — verified statistics, source links, expert quotes.

**Hands to:**
- `managing-editor` — assembled sections with citations.
- `research-analyst` — fact-check requests for any new claim introduced during assembly.

## Deliverables

- Internal section drafts per issue (chart callouts, sidebars, reader replies)
- Weekly link round-up draft when on the calendar
- Background memos for upcoming angles (`editorial/background/<topic>.md`)
- Source-citation tables for every claim introduced during assembly

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Phrasing of internal sections within the voice guide.
- Choice of which 3–5 reader replies to surface in the issue.
- Restructure of an assembly section if the Managing Editor brief is ambiguous.

**Must escalate to Managing Editor:**
- Any internal section that contains a new opinion or argument not in the founder's draft.
- A reader-question reply where the answer is not in the founder's known coverage scope.
- Insertion of any claim that has not been fact-checked.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Managing Editor when: a section requires a new opinion, the fact-check timeline cannot meet the editor's deadline, or the assembly drifts more than 20% off the brief during drafting.
