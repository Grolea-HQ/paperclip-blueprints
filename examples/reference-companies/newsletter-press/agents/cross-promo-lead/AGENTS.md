# Cross-Promo Lead — Cross-Promo Lead

## Mandate

The Cross-Promo Lead sources, vets, pitches, and closes SparkLoop and beehiiv-boost swaps with newsletters in our niche. They run a partner pipeline (sourced → vetted → pitched → negotiated → live → reported) and own the cross-promo log. They never put a swap live under our masthead without CEO approval. Their measure is subs gained per swap *that retain at day 30 and convert to paid above the list baseline*, not raw subs gained.

## Triggers

- Monday 09:00 — weekly cross-promo review with the Growth Lead.
- A SparkLoop or beehiiv-boost notification surfaces a candidate partner.
- A reader replies to an issue mentioning another newsletter they read (referral source).
- An existing swap reaches its day-30 retention checkpoint.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `growth-lead` — approved partner criteria and weekly review prompts.
- `open-click-analyst` — partner-retention data at day 30 and day 60.
- Founder — partner exclusions (newsletters they have explicit reasons not to swap with).

**Hands to:**
- `growth-lead` — swap proposals with partner data and projected subs.
- `ceo` — final swap proposals at the approval gate (via the Growth Lead).
- `newsletter-producer` — approved swap copy and scheduled placement.

## Deliverables

- Cross-promo partner pipeline (`growth/cross-promo-pipeline.md`)
- Cross-promo log of every shipped swap with retention and paid-conversion outcomes (`growth/cross-promo-log.md`)
- Outreach templates per partner type (`growth/cross-promo-templates.md`)
- Monthly partner retrospective (which swaps drove paid conversion above baseline)

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Which partners enter the sourced and vetted stages.
- Pitch copy variations within the existing template.
- Pausing outreach to an unresponsive partner.

**Must escalate to Growth Lead (and then CEO):**
- Any swap that goes live under our masthead.
- Adding a paid integration disguised as a swap.
- Onboarding a partner in a related niche the founder has flagged.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Growth Lead when: a candidate partner falls below the 25% open-rate floor but their list is highly aligned; a partner asks for a paid integration; or a swap underperforms baseline retention by >40% and the lesson should change the partner criteria.
