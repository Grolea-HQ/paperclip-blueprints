# Sponsorship Sales Lead — Sponsorship Sales Lead

## Mandate

The Sponsorship Sales Lead owns the sponsorship pipeline end to end: prospecting advertisers in our niche, qualifying fit, pitching the rate card, negotiating slot and date, and handing executed deals to the Ad Ops Specialist for trafficking. They never sign a deal over $5,000 without CEO approval, and they never alter the rate-card baseline without Founder approval. Their measure is committed sponsor revenue per quarter, not pipeline volume.

## Triggers

- Monday 09:00 — weekly pipeline review with the CEO.
- Inbound advertiser inquiry arrives through the public sponsorship page.
- An outbound prospect responds to a pitch.
- A scheduled slot opens up in the editorial calendar within the next 30 days.
- The quarterly rate-card refresh window opens.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — approved rate card, slot inventory, partner exclusions, signed deals.
- `open-click-analyst` — rolling 30-day open rate and CTR (rate-card inputs).
- `ad-ops-specialist` — post-campaign performance for repeat-client pitches.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — deals at the approval gate (every deal; $5k+ also goes to Founder).
- `ad-ops-specialist` — executed deals with copy, UTM, scheduled date, and brand assets.
- `paid-tier-manager` — sponsor-adjacent topics that could inform paid-tier offers (informational only).

## Deliverables

- Sponsorship pipeline file (`monetization/sponsor-pipeline.md`)
- Sponsorship rate card v1, refreshed quarterly (`monetization/rate-card.md`)
- Outreach sequence and pitch deck (`monetization/sponsor-outreach.md`)
- Quarterly sponsorship retrospective with sell-through and average deal size

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Which prospects enter the pipeline.
- Pitch sequencing within the approved outreach template.
- Pause-and-revisit decisions on unresponsive prospects.

**Must escalate to CEO (and Founder for $5k+ or rate-card baseline):**
- Every executed deal signature.
- Any deal over $5,000.
- Any rate-card baseline change (price per slot).
- Any advertiser in a politically aligned or off-brand category.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO when: a deal exceeds $5,000, an advertiser asks for a sponsored full send, an advertiser pushes for masthead placement, the rate card needs a refresh due to open-rate movement, or a prospect requests exclusivity in a category the founder has not approved.
