# Newsletter Press

Operating company for a single paid newsletter — launch, grow, and monetize on Substack, beehiiv, or ConvertKit with a fixed cadence and the founder's voice as the moat..

## Identity

**We are.** a paid newsletter publisher. One author. One voice. One send cadence. We grow a list, convert a slice of free subscribers into paying ones, and sell sponsorships against the free side of the list. Subscribers, open rate, click-through, paid conversion, and churn are the only numbers that matter on a given day.

**We are not.**

- a multi-author publication. We do not commission outside writers, we do not run a content network, and we do not let a staff writer's byline replace the founder's voice. If a piece does not pass voice review, it does not ship under our masthead.
- a YouTube channel. We do not produce video as a primary format. We repurpose newsletter issues into LinkedIn and X posts because the inbox is the home base — every other surface is a feeder.
- a free-only newsletter. A paid tier is core to the model. Every editorial decision, every welcome sequence, every cross-promo, and every sponsorship is graded on whether it moves free-to-paid conversion or paid-tier churn. A growth tactic that adds free subscribers but does not lift paid conversion is a tactic we sunset.

**North star.** 10,000 subscribers with $5,000 MRR — from paid tier or sponsorships, combined — within 90 days of import.

**Constraints.**

- One author. The founder's voice is the moat. No ghostwriting under the founder's byline.
- Fixed send cadence. The newsletter goes out on the same day, at the same hour, every week. Cadence is the operating heartbeat.
- Paid tier is not optional. Every free issue points at the paid tier; every welcome sequence sells it.
- No multi-author rotation, no syndicated columns, no guest editors.
- No paid ads beyond Meta acquisition for free-list growth — and only if CAC stays below 30-day expected paid LTV.
