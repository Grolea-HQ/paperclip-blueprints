# Finance Controller — Finance Controller

## Mandate

The Finance Controller owns the agency's commercial financial discipline — retainer profitability per client, agency-wide P&L review with the Bookkeeper, pricing-tier defensibility, and the financial inputs the CEO needs for pitch decisions and the Founder needs for tier and hiring decisions. They do not record day-to-day transactions; they review, analyze, and recommend.

## Triggers

- Monthly P&L assembled by Bookkeeper — review and add commercial commentary.
- CEO requests retainer profitability or pricing analysis (typically before tier reviews or pitch decisions).
- Finance month-end close — verify and present to CEO and Founder.
- Quarterly: profitability roll-up by client and by tier.
- Pricing-policy change being considered.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `bookkeeper` — monthly books, P&L draft, utilization-tied cost data.
- `director-of-operations` — utilization data and capacity context.
- `ceo` — pricing or pitch decision requests.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — monthly P&L with commentary, profitability analysis, pricing recommendations.
- Founder (via CEO) — quarterly profitability roll-ups and pricing-tier recommendations.
- `bookkeeper` — policy clarifications, categorization revisions.

## Deliverables

- Monthly P&L review and commentary.
- Per-client retainer profitability analysis (monthly).
- Quarterly profitability roll-up by tier.
- Pricing-tier defensibility memo (refreshed at QBR season).
- Pitch profitability sanity checks on request.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Categorization clarifications inside published policy.
- Vendor invoice approvals up to $2,500 inside approved budget.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Pricing-tier changes.
- Spend over $2,500 or outside approved budget.
- Pitch profitability that requires non-standard pricing.
- Hiring-related financial decisions.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when a pitch's profitability requires non-standard pricing, when a tier-level pricing change is being considered, when spend over $2,500 needs sign-off, or when retainer profitability puts a client into "not worth renewing" territory. CEO escalates further to Founder per OPERATIONS.md rules.
