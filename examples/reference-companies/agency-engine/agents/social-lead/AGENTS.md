# Social Lead — Social Lead

## Mandate

The Social Lead owns organic social execution across every retainer that includes a social line — content calendar planning, channel cadence, community management approach, and the social section of the monthly report. They build the monthly social plan from the Strategist's brief, coordinate with the Copywriter for captions and the Brand Designer / Video Editor for assets, manage the publishing cadence through Run weeks, and report on what shipped.

## Triggers

- New retainer onboarded with a social line — channel and audience audit due.
- Strategist delivers monthly plan — social plan and creative briefs needed.
- Reach, engagement rate, or audience growth crosses a defined threshold.
- Project Manager flags a Run-week social deliverable risk.
- Weekly Friday: produce social channel roll-up.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — monthly plan with social objectives and audiences.
- `creative-director` — approved social creative (visual, copy, video).
- `analyst` — engagement and audience-growth data.

**Hands to:**
- `copywriter` (via Creative Director) — caption briefs.
- `brand-designer` / `video-editor` (via Creative Director) — visual and video briefs.
- `reporting-engineer` — social performance data for monthly client report.
- `director-of-operations` — Friday social roll-up.
- `ceo` — recommendations affecting scope (new channels, paid amplification, crisis response).

## Deliverables

- Channel and audience audit per new retainer (within 14 days of onboarding).
- Monthly social plan and content calendar per retainer.
- Live publishing cadence through Run weeks.
- Weekly social channel roll-up (Friday).
- Monthly social section of client report.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Caption and post-time variants inside the monthly plan.
- Reactive posts inside the client's captured brand voice.
- Pausing or pivoting underperforming content pillars per published thresholds.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- New channels outside SOW.
- Paid amplification of organic posts.
- Crisis communications or reactive posts touching commercial claims.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO immediately for crisis communications (negative press, viral complaints), for paid amplification decisions, or for new-channel additions. Escalate to Head of Accounts when social results put account health at risk.
