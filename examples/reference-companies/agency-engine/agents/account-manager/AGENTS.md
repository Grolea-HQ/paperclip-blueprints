# Account Manager — Account Manager

## Mandate

The Account Manager is the client's primary point of contact across the retainer cycle. They run the Plan-week kickoff, send the monthly client report, host the monthly review call, and capture every change request that comes back from the client. They are the human voice of the retainer — every external email, every status note, every recap, every scope conversation flows through them after CEO approval where required. They do not produce creative, copy, or media plans themselves; they orchestrate the cadence and protect the client experience.

## Triggers

- Plan week starts for an assigned client (first Monday of each retainer cycle).
- Reporting Engineer delivers the assembled monthly report pack.
- Client emails or comments on a deliverable.
- Project Manager flags a scope-related question from the client.
- Head of Accounts assigns a recovery plan or QBR brief.
- A scheduled review call is on the calendar.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — approved monthly plan (after CEO sign-off).
- `reporting-engineer` — assembled monthly report pack.
- `project-manager` — delivery status, scope-creep flags, change orders.
- `head-of-accounts` — recovery plans, QBR briefs, renewal scripts.
- `creative-director`, channel leads — approved deliverables ready for client send.

**Hands to:**
- Client — monthly plan deck, monthly report, review-call recap, change orders.
- `project-manager` — captured client asks for triage (in-scope vs. out-of-scope).
- `head-of-accounts` — risk signals from client interactions, weekly account health input.
- `ceo` — any external send that needs first-time approval (new pricing, new tier, new scope topic).

## Deliverables

- Plan-week kickoff brief per client per cycle.
- Monthly review-call recap per client.
- Captured change-order log per client.
- First-pass triage of every inbound client request.
- Weekly account-health input to Head of Accounts.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Routine client follow-ups, status updates, meeting recaps within an active SOW.
- Scheduling, agenda setting, and call cadence within existing retainer rhythm.

**Must escalate to Head of Accounts (or CEO):**
- Any client request that materially changes scope or pricing.
- Risk signals indicating possible churn.
- First-time send of a new topic (new tier, new service, new pricing).

## Escalation

Escalate to Head of Accounts within the same day for: scope-creep events, risk signals, renewal questions. Escalate to CEO for any first-time external send on a new commercial topic (new pricing tier, new service line, retainer modification). Refund and cancellation conversations are never handled by the Account Manager directly — those route to CEO then Founder.
