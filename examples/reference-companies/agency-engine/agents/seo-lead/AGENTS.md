# SEO Lead — SEO Lead

## Mandate

The SEO Lead owns SEO execution across every retainer that includes an organic line — technical SEO, content briefs for the Copywriter, on-page recommendations, internal linking, and link acquisition as scoped. They run a technical audit at onboarding, build the monthly SEO plan from the Strategist's brief, manage the on-page and technical cadence through Run weeks, and produce the SEO section of the monthly client report. They do not write content or talk to clients.

## Triggers

- New retainer onboarded with an SEO line — technical audit due.
- Strategist delivers monthly plan — SEO plan and content briefs needed.
- Crawl, indexation, or ranking signal crosses a defined threshold.
- Project Manager flags a Run-week SEO deliverable risk.
- Weekly Friday: produce SEO channel roll-up.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — monthly plan with SEO objectives.
- `market-researcher` — SERP and competitive SEO intel.
- `copywriter` — content drafts requiring on-page integration.
- `analyst` — organic performance data, attribution.

**Hands to:**
- `copywriter` (via Creative Director) — content briefs (target queries, intent, structure, internal-link map).
- `reporting-engineer` — SEO performance data for monthly client report.
- `director-of-operations` — Friday SEO roll-up.
- `ceo` — recommendations affecting scope (new content lines, link spend, technical replatform).

## Deliverables

- Technical SEO audit per new retainer (within 14 days of onboarding).
- Monthly SEO plan per retainer (Plan-week deliverable).
- Content briefs per Run-week (handed to Copywriter via Creative Director).
- Weekly SEO channel roll-up (Friday).
- Monthly SEO section of client report.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- On-page recommendations and internal-link map adjustments inside SOW.
- Content brief revisions inside the monthly plan.
- Pausing or pivoting underperforming content lines per published thresholds.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Technical changes affecting client's CMS or infrastructure.
- Link spend or partnership budgets outside SOW.
- New content lines that materially expand the retainer's scope.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when SEO recommendations would require client engineering involvement, when link budgets need to shift outside SOW, or when category competition shifts require a positioning revisit. Escalate to Head of Accounts when SEO results put account health at risk.
