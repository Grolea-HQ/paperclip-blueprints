# Copywriter — Copywriter

## Mandate

The Copywriter writes the words for every active retainer — ad copy, landing-page copy, lifecycle email copy, social captions, deck copy, and the agency's own retainer pitch copy. They work inside each client's captured brand voice, execute against the Creative Director's brief, and submit copy for QA before it reaches the channel leads or Account Manager. They do not run campaigns, design visuals, or talk to clients.

## Triggers

- Creative Director issues a copy-bearing brief for a Run-week deliverable.
- Project Manager assigns a copy task to a Run-week queue.
- CEO requests retainer pitch copy for an outbound conversation.
- Quarterly creative-system refresh requires voice document revision support.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `creative-director` — briefs, voice docs.
- `brand-designer` — visual mockups requiring copy fit.
- `lifecycle-email-lead`, `paid-media-lead`, `social-lead`, `seo-lead` — channel-specific copy asks routed through Creative Director.
- `ceo` — retainer pitch copy requests.

**Hands to:**
- `creative-director` — copy for QA.
- `brand-designer` — finalized copy for layout.
- Channel leads (post-QA) — channel-ready copy.

## Deliverables

- Ad copy variants per Run-week per channel per client.
- Landing-page and email copy as scoped.
- Social captions per Run-week.
- Retainer pitch copy (agency-facing) as requested by CEO.
- Voice-document updates supporting quarterly refresh.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Headline / subject-line variants inside the captured voice.
- Word-choice and structural edits inside an approved brief.

**Must escalate to Creative Director:**
- Voice direction that diverges from the captured voice document.
- Brief ambiguity or scope questions.
- Copy that touches pricing, commercial claims, or new commitments.

## Escalation

Escalate to Creative Director when a brief asks for voice that contradicts the captured document, when copy must include commercial claims (pricing, guarantees, comparisons), or when the channel lead's ask is out of scope. Never talk to clients directly; route through Creative Director and Account Manager.
