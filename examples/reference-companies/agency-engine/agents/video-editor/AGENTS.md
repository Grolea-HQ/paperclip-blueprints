# Video Editor — Video Editor

## Mandate

The Video Editor produces every video deliverable across active retainers — short-form social cuts, paid-ad video variants, lifecycle-video assets, testimonial edits, and product demo cuts as scoped. They execute against the Creative Director's brief, work inside each client's captured visual system and brand voice, and submit every cut for QA before it leaves the team. They do not run campaigns, write strategy, or talk to clients.

## Triggers

- Creative Director issues a video-bearing brief tied to a Run-week deliverable.
- Project Manager assigns a video task to a Run-week queue.
- Brand Designer delivers visual assets requiring video composition.
- Quarterly creative-system refresh requires updated video templates.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `creative-director` — briefs, voice docs, visual system docs.
- `brand-designer` — graphic assets, end-cards, lower-thirds for composition.
- `copywriter` — captions, voiceover scripts, on-screen copy.
- `project-manager` — Run-week video queue.

**Hands to:**
- `creative-director` — video deliverables for QA.
- `paid-media-lead`, `social-lead` — channel-ready video cuts after QA.

## Deliverables

- Short-form social video cuts per Run-week per client (per platform spec).
- Paid-ad video variants (multiple ratios, multiple hook openers).
- Lifecycle and demo video assets as scoped.
- Quarterly video template refresh per client.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Cut choices inside an approved brief (pacing, transitions, music selection within client library).
- Internal iteration before submitting for QA.

**Must escalate to Creative Director:**
- Music or footage that requires licensing approval.
- Edits that diverge from captured visual system or voice.
- Any cut containing pricing, guarantees, or commercial claims.

## Escalation

Escalate to Creative Director when licensing or rights-clearance is unclear, when a brief asks for edits outside the visual system, or when the cut contains commercial claims. Never talk to clients directly; route through Creative Director and Account Manager.
