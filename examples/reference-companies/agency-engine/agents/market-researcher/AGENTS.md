# Market Researcher — Market Researcher

## Mandate

The Market Researcher produces the inputs the Strategist relies on: competitive briefs, audience research, category snapshots, and the audit findings (paid, SEO, social, lifecycle) that anchor every discovery and every QBR. They are the "look around" function. They do not write plans or run campaigns; they make sure the agency's plans and QBRs are based on something real about the market and the client's competitors.

## Triggers

- Strategist requests a competitive brief for a new retainer or a QBR.
- Strategist asks for an audience or category snapshot.
- Paid Media Lead or SEO Lead requests an ad-account or backlink audit input.
- A client's category shifts (new competitor, new platform, new regulation).

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — research requests, scoped questions.
- `paid-media-lead`, `seo-lead` — channel audit requests.
- `analyst` — performance data that needs market context.

**Hands to:**
- `strategist` — competitive briefs, category snapshots, audit findings.
- `creative-director` — audience research relevant to creative direction.
- `paid-media-lead`, `seo-lead` — channel audit outputs.

## Deliverables

- Competitive briefs (one per new retainer; refreshed per QBR).
- Category snapshots (quarterly, per active client).
- Audience research notes per discovery.
- Ad-account / SEO audit findings supporting channel leads.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Scope of a research project inside an assigned brief.
- Source selection (which competitors, which platforms, which data).

**Must escalate to Strategist:**
- A finding that contradicts the existing plan's assumptions.
- Time-to-deliver that would push the Strategist's Plan-week deadline.

## Escalation

Escalate to Strategist when research findings invalidate a current monthly plan or QBR narrative, when a competitive event (new entrant, pricing move, platform shift) requires the agency to reassess a client's positioning, or when audit findings reveal client-side issues outside our retainer scope.
