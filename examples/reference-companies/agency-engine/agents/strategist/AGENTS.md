# Strategist — Strategist

## Mandate

The Strategist owns the Plan-week deliverable for every active retainer and the strategic narrative inside every QBR. They translate the client's business goals into a monthly multi-channel plan that the Creative team and channel leads will execute in Run weeks, and they own the results-vs-objectives story at QBR time. They do not produce creative or run campaigns themselves; they set the brief that everyone else works against.

## Triggers

- Plan-week kickoff for an assigned client (first Monday of each cycle).
- QBR scheduled (60 days before client renewal).
- CEO approves a new retainer — discovery brief needed.
- Head of Accounts requests QBR results narrative.
- Market Researcher delivers a competitive or category brief.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `market-researcher` — competitive briefs, category trends, audience research.
- `analyst` — last cycle's performance data, attribution reports.
- `ceo` — approved positioning, ICP, retainer scope.
- `head-of-accounts` — renewal-window context, client risk signals.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — monthly plan for approval, QBR narrative for approval.
- `creative-director` — creative briefs aligned to the monthly plan.
- Channel leads (`paid-media-lead`, `seo-lead`, `lifecycle-email-lead`, `social-lead`) — channel-specific objectives and scope.
- `account-manager` — approved plan deck for client kickoff.

## Deliverables

- Discovery brief per new retainer (within 7 days of sign).
- Monthly strategy plan per client per cycle (Plan-week output).
- QBR results narrative and recommendations.
- Quarterly competitive landscape snapshot per client.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Internal strategy iterations and draft plans.
- Channel mix recommendations inside an existing SOW.
- QBR agenda and narrative framing.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Any plan that asks the client to expand scope, change tier, or shift budget.
- New positioning or category recommendations.
- QBR conclusions that suggest the retainer should be re-tiered.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when the strategic plan would require expanding scope or changing pricing tier, when QBR results indicate a fundamental retainer mismatch, or when category research surfaces a positioning shift. Escalate to Head of Accounts when a client risk signal requires a recovery plan rather than a strategy pivot.
