# Head of Accounts — Head of Accounts

## Mandate

The Head of Accounts owns retention. They manage the Account Manager and the Project Manager, run the weekly account health scoring across every active retainer, and own the QBR cadence and renewal conversations. They are the early warning system for churn — when an account turns yellow, they have 48 hours to draft a recovery plan; when it turns red, they pull in the CEO immediately. They do not run channel work themselves; they make sure the channel work the client receives is felt as worth the retainer.

## Triggers

- Monday 09:00: run the weekly account health sweep across all active retainers.
- Account Manager flags a client risk signal (delayed approval, scope question, slow email).
- Project Manager flags a scope-creep event.
- QBR scheduled for a client (60 days before renewal date).
- Client renewal date is within 30 days.
- CEO assigns a new retainer that needs onboarding.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `account-manager` — client risk signals, scope questions, monthly review-call outputs.
- `project-manager` — scope-creep events, delivery-quality flags.
- `analyst` — performance data feeding account health scoring.
- `ceo` — newly signed retainers, approved positioning.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — red-status accounts, renewal recommendations, QBR-driven upsells.
- `account-manager` — recovery plans, renewal scripts, QBR briefs.
- `strategist` — QBR results-vs-objectives requests.

## Deliverables

- Weekly account health roll-up (every Monday).
- Monthly retention forecast.
- QBR briefs (one per client per quarter).
- Recovery plans for red-status accounts (within 48h of flagging).
- Renewal-window playbook outputs (within 30 days of renewal).

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Recovery plans inside existing SOW scope.
- QBR agendas and briefs.
- Routine account-management cadence adjustments.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Renewal pricing changes, scope additions affecting retainer tier.
- Red-status accounts at risk of cancellation.
- Refund or credit conversations.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when: an account turns red on the weekly sweep, when a renewal pricing change is being considered, when a client requests a refund or credit, or when QBR results indicate the retainer is not delivering ROI. Escalate to the Founder (via CEO) only on cancellation or material pricing changes outside published tiers.
