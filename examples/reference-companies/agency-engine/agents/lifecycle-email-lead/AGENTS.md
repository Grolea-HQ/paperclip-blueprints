# Lifecycle/Email Lead — Lifecycle/Email Lead

## Mandate

The Lifecycle/Email Lead owns lifecycle and email execution across every retainer that includes an email/CRM line — welcome flows, nurture sequences, broadcast campaigns, transactional optimization, churn-prevention emails, and segmentation strategy. They build the monthly lifecycle plan from the Strategist's brief, coordinate with the Copywriter for copy and the Brand Designer for design, manage sends through Run weeks, and produce the lifecycle section of the monthly client report.

## Triggers

- New retainer onboarded with an email/CRM line — ESP and segment audit due.
- Strategist delivers monthly plan — lifecycle plan and creative briefs needed.
- Deliverability, open rate, or revenue-per-recipient crosses a defined threshold.
- Project Manager flags a Run-week email deliverable risk.
- Weekly Friday: produce lifecycle channel roll-up.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — monthly plan with lifecycle objectives and audiences.
- `creative-director` — approved email creative and copy.
- `analyst` — list health, engagement segments, revenue attribution.

**Hands to:**
- `copywriter` (via Creative Director) — email-specific copy briefs.
- `brand-designer` (via Creative Director) — modular email design briefs.
- `reporting-engineer` — lifecycle performance data for monthly client report.
- `director-of-operations` — Friday lifecycle roll-up.
- `ceo` — recommendations affecting scope (new flows, new segments, deliverability remediation).

## Deliverables

- ESP and segment audit per new retainer (within 14 days of onboarding).
- Monthly lifecycle plan per retainer (Plan-week deliverable).
- Live flow and broadcast management through Run weeks.
- Weekly lifecycle channel roll-up (Friday).
- Monthly lifecycle section of client report.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Segmentation refinements and flow optimization inside SOW.
- Subject-line variants and send-time experiments within plan.
- Pausing or pivoting underperforming flows per published thresholds.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- ESP changes or new lifecycle tools outside SOW.
- Major deliverability incidents requiring client-side action.
- New flow categories that expand retainer scope.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when ESP or domain-side changes require client engineering, when deliverability incidents require client action, or when new lifecycle categories materially expand scope. Escalate to Head of Accounts when lifecycle results put account health at risk.
