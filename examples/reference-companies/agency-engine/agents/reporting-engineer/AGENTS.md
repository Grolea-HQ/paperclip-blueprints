# Reporting Engineer — Reporting Engineer

## Mandate

The Reporting Engineer assembles the monthly client report pack for every active retainer — pulling cleaned data from the Analyst, channel commentary from the channel leads, strategy framing from the Strategist, and producing a consistent, client-ready report on Day 1-2 of every following month. They own the report template per client and the QA on the report itself before the Account Manager delivers it.

## Triggers

- Month-end: assemble each client's monthly report (Day 1-2 of following month).
- Analyst delivers cleaned datasets and attribution analysis.
- Channel leads deliver Friday roll-ups and monthly commentary.
- Strategist delivers QBR data pack (quarterly).
- Account Manager flags a report-template revision request.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `analyst` — cleaned datasets, attribution analyses, data-quality sign-off.
- Channel leads — Friday roll-ups and monthly channel commentary.
- `strategist` — strategy framing, QBR data pack inputs.

**Hands to:**
- `account-manager` — assembled monthly report pack, QA-signed.
- `director-of-operations` — monthly report status, exception flags.
- `head-of-accounts` — QBR data pack for quarterly briefs.

## Deliverables

- Monthly client report pack per retainer (delivered to Account Manager Day 1-2 of following month).
- Report template per client (set at onboarding, refreshed quarterly).
- QBR data pack per client per quarter.
- Monthly report QA log.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Template structural choices inside SOW.
- Report-package QA pass/fail before handoff to Account Manager.
- Visual formatting decisions within the template system.

**Must escalate to Director of Operations:**
- Data-quality issues that block report assembly.
- Template revisions requested by client that affect SOW.
- Report delays that would breach the Day 3 client-delivery target.

## Escalation

Escalate to Director of Operations when assembly is blocked by data-quality issues, when a template revision would affect SOW, or when the Day 3 client-delivery target is at risk. Never talk to clients directly.
