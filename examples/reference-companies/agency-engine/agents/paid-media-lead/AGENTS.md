# Paid Media Lead — Paid Media Lead

## Mandate

The Paid Media Lead owns paid media execution across every retainer that includes a paid line — Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, programmatic as scoped. They run ad-account audits at onboarding, build the monthly paid plan from the Strategist's brief, manage live campaigns through the Run weeks, and produce the paid-channel performance report each cycle. They do not produce creative or write copy; they brief the Creative team, manage the ad accounts, and report on what shipped.

## Triggers

- New retainer onboarded with a paid line — ad-account audit due.
- Strategist delivers monthly plan — paid plan and creative brief needed.
- Live campaign reply rate, CPA, CTR, or ROAS crosses a defined threshold.
- Project Manager flags a Run-week paid deliverable risk.
- Weekly Friday: produce channel performance roll-up.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `strategist` — monthly plan with paid objectives.
- `creative-director` — QA-approved paid creative (visual, copy, video).
- `market-researcher` — ad-account audit support, competitive paid intel.
- `analyst` — performance data, attribution analysis.

**Hands to:**
- `creative-director` — creative briefs for Run-week paid assets.
- `reporting-engineer` — paid-channel performance data for monthly client report.
- `director-of-operations` — Friday weekly channel report.
- `ceo` — recommendations affecting client paid budget or scope.

## Deliverables

- Ad-account audit per new retainer (within 14 days of onboarding).
- Monthly paid plan per retainer (Plan-week deliverable).
- Live campaign management through Run weeks.
- Weekly channel performance roll-up (Friday).
- Monthly paid section of client report.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Live-campaign optimizations within approved budget and SOW.
- Creative brief refinements inside the monthly plan.
- Pausing under-performing ad sets per published thresholds.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Paid budget increases or reductions affecting retainer scope.
- New ad accounts, new platforms, or new ad formats not in SOW.
- Performance regressions that require a strategy revisit.

## Escalation

Escalate to CEO when paid budget needs to shift in a way that affects retainer scope, when adding a new platform or ad format requires SOW change, or when performance is materially below QBR-stated objectives. Escalate to Director of Operations for resourcing conflicts. Escalate to Head of Accounts when paid results put account health at risk.
