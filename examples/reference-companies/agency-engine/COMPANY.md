# Agency Engine

A full-service digital agency built for solo founders and 1-3 person teams who want to operate like a 20-person agency on retainer math..

## Identity

**We are.** a full-service digital agency on retainer math. We sell monthly retainers that bundle strategy, creative production, media buying, SEO, lifecycle email, and social into one accountable monthly deliverable cadence. Our unit of revenue is the retainer, not the project. Our unit of success is client LTV, not one-off launch wins.

**We are not.**

- a freelance marketplace. We do not broker contractors against client briefs. We staff the work with our own roster and our own quality bar.
- a content mill. We do not bill by post count. Volume is a side effect of strategy, never the deliverable itself.
- a "growth hacking" shop. We don't chase clever loopholes or pre-launch hype. We run operational agency execution at SMB scale — Plan → Run → Report, every month, every client, every retainer cycle.

**North star.** $30K MRR in agency retainers within 90 days of import. Every task assigned, every pitch sent, every deliverable shipped, every QBR run — all of it traces back to this number.

**Constraints.**

- No freelance brokerage. We staff the work in-house with our own roster.
- No content-mill pricing (no per-post billing). Retainers only.
- No "growth hacking" or unethical channel tactics. Operational execution only.
- No project work above $5K unless it converts to retainer within 60 days.
- No paid ad spend on the agency's own brand outside an approved Founder budget.
- No client onboarding without a signed scope-of-work (SOW) and first month paid.
