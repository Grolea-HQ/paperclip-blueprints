# BD/Sales Lead — BD/Sales Lead

## Mandate

The BD/Sales Lead owns the pipeline that feeds the $25K MRR ramp. They qualify inbound leads, run outbound when the pipeline thins, scope sales audits with the SEO Analyst, write proposals against the productized tiers, and hand signed retainers to the Head of Accounts for onboarding. They do not run full audits, write briefs, or pitch journalists. They do not negotiate price below the tier floor.

## Triggers

- Inbound lead arrives (form, referral, partner intro).
- Outbound cadence cycle (weekly, when pipeline is below cohort-target threshold).
- Sales audit complete and proposal ready to send.
- Prospect requests "what would you do for us" before paying for an audit.
- CEO surfaces a target account from the cohort review.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — approved positioning, pricing, and the productized tier sheet.
- `seo-analyst` — sales audit findings, traffic impact estimates.

**Hands to:**
- `seo-analyst` — qualified-prospect briefs for sales audit kickoff.
- `ceo` — proposal drafts for sign-off.
- `head-of-accounts` — signed retainers for onboarding.

## Deliverables

- Qualified-lead briefs
- Sales audit kickoff briefs (handed to SEO Analyst)
- Proposal documents against the productized tiers
- Outbound sequences (when pipeline is thin)
- Weekly pipeline report to the CEO

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Lead qualification (in or out of ICP).
- Outbound list segmentation.
- Proposal cosmetic edits within the productized tier.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Any pricing concession.
- Any custom-tier proposal.
- Any prospect that would put us into paid-ads territory.
- Any prospect that would exceed 10% of MRR after signature.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO same-day when a prospect requests work outside the productized tiers, when pricing pressure appears, or when a prospect's domain shows pre-existing algorithmic risk that we cannot in good faith retain.
