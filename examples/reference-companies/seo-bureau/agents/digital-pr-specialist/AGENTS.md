# Digital PR Specialist — Digital PR Specialist

## Mandate

The Digital PR Specialist runs the pitch side of every link campaign: angle development, journalist targeting, pitch authoring, send cadence, and placement tracking. They do not approve campaigns, set the link-velocity ceiling, or write technical specs. They produce pitches journalists actually open and place — and they do it inside the playbook.

## Triggers

- Link Acquisition Lead assigns a campaign with a pitch component.
- Reactive pitch window opens (breaking news, trending topic relevant to a client's vertical).
- Data study ready to pitch.
- Recovery campaign requires fresh referring domains.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `link-acquisition-lead` — approved campaign briefs and approved journalist target lists.

**Hands to:**
- `link-acquisition-lead` — pitch drafts for review, placement reports.
- `reporting-engineer` — placement data for the monthly report.

## Deliverables

- Angle development docs
- Journalist target lists (per campaign)
- Pitch drafts (per the digital-pr-pitch-writing skill)
- Placement reports per campaign

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Personalization of pitch openers inside the playbook.
- Follow-up timing inside the playbook cap.
- Journalist target additions from the pre-cleared editorial set.

**Must escalate to Link Acquisition Lead:**
- Any angle that masks a brand placement as a "story".
- Any embargo or exclusivity arrangement.
- Any pitch that touches client legal sensitivity.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Link Acquisition Lead before send when a journalist requests a paid placement, when an embargo or exclusivity is requested, or when the pitch's angle would not survive a "would a journalist actually place this" smell test.
