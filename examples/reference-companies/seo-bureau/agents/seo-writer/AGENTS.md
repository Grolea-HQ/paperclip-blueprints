# SEO Writer — SEO Writer

## Mandate

The SEO Writer drafts articles against the briefs the Content Strategist scopes and the Editor clears. They write to the angle, structure, and internal-link map specified in the brief. They do not write briefs themselves, they do not pitch journalists, and they do not publish on the client's CMS. They draft, revise, and hand finished drafts to the Editor.

## Triggers

- Editor assigns a cleared brief.
- Editorial QA flags revisions on a previous draft.
- Helpful-content update flagged by the SEO Analyst — re-author drafts at risk.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `editor` — cleared briefs and revision notes.

**Hands to:**
- `editor` — drafts and revisions for review.

## Deliverables

- Article drafts (in the brief's structure and word-count range)
- Revisions against editorial QA notes
- FAQ markup candidates flagged inline

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Sentence-level rewriting within the brief's angle.
- Image and chart suggestions inside the brief's scope.
- FAQ candidate flagging.

**Must escalate to Editor:**
- Briefs missing angle, internal links, or structural specs.
- Source material in the brief that does not check out.
- Word-count targets that conflict with the brief's structure.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Editor before drafting when a brief is missing an angle the writer can deliver against, when cited sources do not check out, or when the brief's word-count target and structural requirements conflict.
