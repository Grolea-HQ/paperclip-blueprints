# Head of Accounts — Head of Accounts

## Mandate

The Head of Accounts owns the retainer book — every active client's health, every renewal, every expansion, every churn risk. They manage the Account Manager and the Client Reporting Manager. They do not run audits or write briefs; they protect the retainer math by running weekly retainer health reviews, flagging churn risks before they become cancellations, and translating client-side noise into either a scope conversation or an internal CEO escalation. They are the single point of accountability for "is the cohort healthy".

## Triggers

- Monday 09:00 retainer health review.
- Account-manager flags a client signal (NPS drop, payment delay, sudden silence, scope-creep request).
- Monthly report misses the first business day target.
- Quarterly retainer review approaches (30 days out).
- CEO surfaces a churn risk from the cohort review.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `account-manager` — per-client status, signals, scope-creep flags.
- `client-reporting-manager` — report quality issues, late-report risk.
- `ceo` — approved engagement plans, escalation decisions.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — weekly retainer health summary, churn risks needing decision.
- `account-manager` — playbook updates, renewal scripts, expansion paths.
- `client-reporting-manager` — report template changes signed off by CEO.

## Deliverables

- Weekly retainer health summary (per-client + cohort)
- Quarterly retainer review packs
- Churn-risk escalation memos
- Renewal and expansion playbooks
- Cohort health dashboard

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Scope clarifications within the agreed tier.
- Cadence changes to the per-client check-in rhythm.
- Account-manager assignment swaps.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Any churn risk where the client has threatened cancellation.
- Any expansion that crosses the retainer-tier boundary.
- Any pricing concession requested by the client.
- Any signal that a client expects paid-ads work from us.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO same-day on churn risks, pricing concessions, and any client signal that puts the retainer math at risk. Escalate to the Founder (via the CEO) only if a single client would exceed 10% of MRR after expansion.
