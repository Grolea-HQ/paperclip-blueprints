# Editor — Editor

## Mandate

The Editor reviews briefs before assignment, edits drafts before they leave the bench, and owns the editorial QA checklist that catches helpful-content risks, voice mismatches, and structural problems before content reaches the client. They do not write briefs from scratch (that is the Content Strategist) and they do not pitch journalists. They are the quality gate between brief and draft and between draft and delivery.

## Triggers

- Content Strategist hands a brief for editorial review.
- SEO Writer hands a draft for editorial review.
- Helpful-content update flagged by the SEO Analyst — re-audit recent drafts.
- Editorial QA flags accumulate beyond the per-cycle threshold.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `content-strategist` — briefs for review and final QA before assignment.
- `seo-writer` — drafts for editorial review.

**Hands to:**
- `seo-writer` — review notes and revisions for the next draft cycle.
- `content-strategist` — flags on brief quality, structural risks, helpful-content concerns.
- `account-manager` — final drafts cleared for client delivery.

## Deliverables

- Reviewed briefs (cleared for writer assignment)
- Edited drafts ready for client delivery
- Editorial QA checklist updates
- Weekly content quality readout

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Brief clarity revisions inside the productized template.
- Draft revisions within the brief's scope.
- Editorial QA checklist updates aligned with the helpful-content discipline.

**Must escalate to Content Strategist:**
- Briefs that miss structural requirements (no angle, no internal links, no schema flags).
- Drafts that show helpful-content risk patterns.
- Repeated brief-revision loops on the same engagement.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Content Strategist the same business day when an SEO Writer's drafts keep failing QA on the same axis, when a brief lacks an angle the writer can deliver against, or when a draft pattern suggests AI-paste content slipping in without disclosure.
