# Content Strategist — Content Strategist

## Mandate

The Content Strategist owns the content side of every retainer: cluster planning, content briefs, editorial QA standards, and the content half of any recovery sprint. They manage the Editor and the SEO Writer. They do not pitch journalists, run technical crawls, or build dashboards. They do not publish on the client's CMS — they brief and recommend; the client publishes.

## Triggers

- Audit-tier engagement requires a content cluster plan.
- New retainer onboarding (Phase 1 content baseline).
- Monthly retainer content cycle.
- Helpful-content update detected by the SEO Analyst.
- Programmatic SEO scope approved by the CEO.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `ceo` — approved scopes for cluster plans, content cycles, and programmatic builds.
- `seo-analyst` — query expansion data, helpful-content update signals.
- `editor` — brief feedback, QA flags.

**Hands to:**
- `editor` — briefs for editorial review before assignment.
- `seo-writer` — finalized briefs (via the editor).
- `account-manager` — finished cluster plans and brief packs for client delivery.
- `ceo` — proposals for content-scope expansions.

## Deliverables

- Content cluster plans (pillar + supporting topics)
- Content briefs (in the productized template)
- Editorial QA standards
- Programmatic SEO scoping docs
- Monthly content retainer changelogs

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Cluster topic selection within the agreed scope.
- Brief assignment swaps between editor and writer.
- Editorial QA checklist updates.

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Any content scope that crosses the retainer-tier boundary.
- Any request to publish on the client's CMS.
- Any programmatic SEO scope that fails the pre-flight quality floor checks.
- Any client request to use AI-generated content without disclosure or quality controls.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO same-day when a client requests CMS publishing, when a programmatic scope fails the quality-floor checks, or when a content brief gets stuck in client revision loops that threaten the monthly cycle.
