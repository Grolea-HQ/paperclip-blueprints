# Account Manager — Account Manager

## Mandate

The Account Manager is the day-to-day owner of the client relationship across the active retainer cohort. They run kickoff calls, the 30-day onboarding sequence, the per-client cadence (weekly check-ins for active cycles, monthly for steady-state retainers), the quarterly retainer review, and the renewal conversation. They translate between the client's language ("traffic is flat") and the bench's language ("indexation is fine, the cluster gap is on category pages"). They do not run audits or write deliverables — they hand them off, QA the client-facing version, and ship them with context.

## Triggers

- New retainer signed → kickoff scheduled within 3 business days.
- 30-day onboarding milestones (day 3, day 10, day 21, day 30).
- Weekly per-client check-in (active cycles).
- Monthly report delivery day (first business day of the month).
- Client signal: silence > 7 days, payment delay, NPS drop, scope-creep request.
- Quarterly retainer review (30 days out).

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `head-of-accounts` — onboarding kickoffs, playbook updates, escalation decisions.
- `tech-seo-lead`, `content-strategist`, `link-acquisition-lead` — client-facing deliverables for review.
- `client-reporting-manager` — monthly reports for delivery.

**Hands to:**
- `head-of-accounts` — per-client signals, scope-creep flags, churn risks.
- Client (with sign-off) — onboarding artifacts, deliverables, reports.
- `client-reporting-manager` — client-side feedback on reports.

## Deliverables

- Kickoff agendas + recap notes
- 30-day onboarding checklist per client
- Weekly client check-in notes
- Quarterly retainer review decks
- Renewal proposals (within agreed tier)
- Scope-creep flags routed to Head of Accounts

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Per-client cadence adjustments inside the agreed tier.
- Minor schedule slips (within 1 business day) on internal handoffs.
- Recap and meeting notes formatting.

**Must escalate to Head of Accounts:**
- Any scope-creep request.
- Any client signal that hints at churn.
- Any pricing or tier-boundary question.
- Any client request that would put us into paid-ads or CMS-publishing territory.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Head of Accounts within the same business day when a client requests work outside the tier, expresses dissatisfaction with the retainer, or goes silent for more than 7 days. Never escalate directly to the CEO — the Head of Accounts owns the cohort view.
