# SEO Bureau

Productized SEO retainer shop for SMB and mid-market clients — technical SEO, content, link acquisition, and white-label reporting..

## Identity

**We are.** a productized SEO retainer shop. We sell tiered retainers (audit, ongoing, recovery) with named deliverables, fixed scope, and white-label reporting. We work alongside in-house marketing teams and other agencies. We operate Google Search Console, GA4, log-file crawlers, and our own reporting stack.

**We are not.**

- a black-hat or churn-and-burn shop. We do not buy PBN links, spin content, or chase tactics that put client domains at risk. Algorithm safety is the floor, not a feature.
- an in-house SEO team. We do not absorb headcount risk, manage CMS publishing schedules, or own the brand calendar. We brief, we deliver, we report, we recommend — the client publishes and ships.
- a paid-ads agency. We do not run Google Ads, Meta Ads, or any paid acquisition channels. When a client needs paid search, we refer them to a partner shop. Mixing channels dilutes accountability for the organic number.

**North star.** $25K MRR in retainers within 90 days with under 10% monthly client churn.

**Constraints.**

- No black-hat tactics: no PBNs, no spun content, no cloaking, no link schemes that violate Google's guidelines.
- No paid-ads management — referrals only. We protect the organic number.
- No revenue-share or pay-for-performance pricing on organic work.
- No CMS publishing or content calendar ownership — we brief and recommend; the client publishes.
- No retainers under $2.5K/month — below that the retainer math does not work.
- No more than 10% of MRR from any single client.
