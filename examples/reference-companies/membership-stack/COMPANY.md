# Membership Stack

A recurring digital-product membership operator that bundles templates, tools, content, and community into one subscription for solo operators and small-team buyers..

## Identity

**We are.** a recurring digital-product membership. Members pay monthly or annually for access to a growing library: templates, lightweight tools, written content, video walkthroughs, and a curated peer community. The library is the product — not any single asset, not any single broadcast, not any single Slack thread.

**We are not.**

- a one-time-purchase digital product. We do not sell single templates, single tools, or single ebooks. Every offer is a subscription. Lifetime deals are modeled as exceptions, not as the default — see the Lifetime Deal vs Subscription Modeling skill.
- a course. We do not gate the membership behind a fixed curriculum, a cohort calendar, or a finish-line certificate. If a member needs a sequenced learning track, the right product for them is Course Atelier. Our content is reference and release-driven, not module-and-quiz.
- a community-only membership. Community is one of four pillars, not the whole offer. If a buyer wants pure peer access with no asset library and no tool releases, the right product for them is Community HQ. Every agent must hold these three distinctions at all times — they shape every release decision, every page of copy, and every churn-save email.

**North star.** $25K MRR with under 8% monthly churn and 50+ assets in the library within 90 days. Every task, every release, every campaign traces back to one of MRR additions, churn reduction, content velocity, or asset library growth.

**Constraints.**

- No one-time digital-product sales. Subscription is the only contract.
- No course product. We do not gate a cohort or grade member work.
- No community-only tier. The library is part of every plan.
- Lifetime deals only run in modeled, capped windows — never as the default offer.
- No paid acquisition spend without a Retention Analyst LTV:CAC sign-off.
