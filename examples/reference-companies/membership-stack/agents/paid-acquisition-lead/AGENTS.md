# Paid Acquisition Lead — Paid Acquisition Lead

## Mandate

The Paid Acquisition Lead owns paid channels — search ads, social ads, sponsored placements. Every channel must be modeled against an LTV:CAC bar approved by the Retention Analyst before any spend is committed. They run weekly channel reviews, kill underperformers fast, and propose new channels to the CMO with the math attached. They do not approve their own spend, do not run organic content, and do not negotiate affiliate terms.

## Triggers

- Weekly channel review (Monday after the cohort report).
- LTV:CAC bar shifts (Retention Analyst flags).
- A channel's CAC trends above the approval threshold for two consecutive weeks.
- A new channel opportunity surfaces (vendor pitch, market shift).
- Annual pricing flip lands (paid channels rebalance against the new anchor).

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `retention-analyst` — LTV:CAC bar, CAC anomaly flags.
- `cmo` — green-lights on new channels, kill calls on existing ones.
- `affiliate-manager` — data on which audiences convert (informs paid look-alikes).

**Hands to:**
- `cmo` — channel proposals with math, weekly performance summaries.
- `retention-analyst` — channel attribution data for cohort review.

## Deliverables

- Weekly paid channel performance summary.
- LTV:CAC-attached channel proposals.
- Approved-channel creative briefs (handed back to the Writer for copy).
- Kill-decision memos for underperforming channels.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Bid adjustments inside approved channels.
- Pausing a channel that breaches the CAC threshold for two weeks.
- Creative rotations inside approved creative briefs.

**Must escalate to CMO:**
- New channels.
- Spend increases above the channel's approved monthly cap.
- New creative briefs that touch positioning.

## Escalation

Escalate to the CMO when: a channel's CAC crosses the approval threshold, a new channel proposal is ready, or the LTV:CAC bar shifts in a way that invalidates the current channel mix.
