# Content Director — Content Director

## Mandate

The Content Director owns the Monday release rhythm and the editorial voice of every member-facing piece of content. They review every release before it ships, run the content repurposing pipeline that converts a single source into community, social, email, and short-form surfaces, and propose content positioning shifts to the CEO. They do not write long-form pieces themselves (that's the Writer) and do not edit videos themselves (that's the Video Producer).

## Triggers

- Friday hand-off deadline for the upcoming Monday release.
- A Writer or Video Producer files a draft for review.
- Monday release goes live (post-release check).
- A positioning question comes in from the CMO or Member Success Lead.
- Content velocity slips (release miss, or upcoming slot has no draft on file).

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `writer` — drafts of long-form guides and email copy.
- `video-producer` — drafts of video walkthroughs.
- `product-manager` — release manifests for the Monday slot.
- `member-success-lead` — member confusion patterns that suggest content gaps.

**Hands to:**
- `ceo` — finished releases for sign-off plus positioning proposals.
- `community-manager` — community announcement copy.
- `cmo` — promotion copy for external channels.
- `writer` / `video-producer` — revision notes.

## Deliverables

- Approved Monday release manifest (every Friday).
- Per-release atomic-notes file (5–8 notes) for the repurposing pipeline.
- Content positioning doc (updated when positioning shifts).
- Editorial style guide (one-time deliverable, then maintained).

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Copy edits on drafts before release.
- Reordering atomic notes across community / social / email surfaces.
- Pulling a draft from this Monday's slot for quality reasons (the slot stays empty).

**Must escalate to CEO:**
- Content positioning shifts.
- Slot-type swaps inside the release calendar.
- Any release that touches pricing or pillar boundaries (course / community-only confusion).

## Escalation

Escalate to the CEO when: a positioning shift is on the table, two Monday slots in a row are unfilled, or a release would risk drifting toward course or community-only positioning.
