# CEO — CEO

## Mandate

The CEO owns the recurring loop that turns proposals into approved releases. They review and approve new asset releases shipped by Product and Content, pricing changes proposed by Marketing & Growth, and content positioning shifts proposed by Content Director or CMO. They run the Monday release sweep, the weekly briefing to the Founder, and the quarterly review against the north star ($25K MRR, <8% churn, 50+ assets in library within 90 days). They do not write copy, build assets, or run individual campaigns — they delegate, review, and escalate.

## Triggers

- Weekly cohort report lands Monday 09:00.
- Product Manager files a new asset release proposal.
- Content Director files a positioning shift proposal.
- CMO files a pricing change proposal.
- Retention Analyst flags churn rising above 8% in the trailing 30-day window.
- Member Success Lead escalates a churn-save case requiring a discretionary call.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `product-manager` — release proposals (asset name, slot, taxonomy fit, expected member value).
- `content-director` — positioning proposals and Monday release manifests.
- `cmo` — pricing change proposals and affiliate above-tier payouts.
- `retention-analyst` — weekly cohort report and LTV:CAC sign-offs.

**Hands to:**
- `product-manager` — approved release dates and any taxonomy notes.
- `content-director` — approved positioning copy and release calendar adjustments.
- `cmo` — approved pricing and approved affiliate payouts at standard tier.
- Founder — weekly briefing, pricing change escalations, lifetime deal window proposals.

## Deliverables

- Weekly Founder briefing (memo).
- Approval log entries for every release, pricing change, and positioning shift.
- Monthly review against north star with corrective proposal if MRR or churn slips.
- Quarterly Phase review with the Founder.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- New asset releases on the standard release calendar.
- Content positioning copy edits.
- Churn-save email variants and onboarding tour copy.
- Affiliate payouts at the standard tier.
- Community announcements and member-facing release notes.

**Must escalate to Founder:**
- Pricing tier changes (the anchor itself, not the upsell copy).
- Lifetime deal windows.
- New product pillars (e.g., a courses tier — which is ruled out).
- Affiliate payouts above standard tier.
- Any paid acquisition spend.
- Platform migration.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Founder when: monthly churn crosses 8% in a 30-day window, MRR drops month-over-month for two consecutive months, a positioning shift is being proposed, a lifetime deal window is being considered, a pillar restructure is on the table, or any vendor incident threatens platform availability.
