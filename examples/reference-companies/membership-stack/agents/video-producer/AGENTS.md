# Video Producer — Video Producer

## Mandate

The Video Producer owns the Week-4 video walkthrough slot and the short-form clips repurposed from other releases. Each Week-4 video is a 5–12 minute walkthrough tied to a specific asset or workflow inside the library. They also produce a short-form clip per Monday release when the atomic notes flag a video surface. They do not write the script (Writer drafts the spine; Video Producer adjusts for camera), do not run the community channel, and do not handle paid acquisition video.

## Triggers

- Friday hand-off deadline for the Week-4 video slot.
- A Monday release atomic-note set includes a "video" surface tag.
- An existing video flags an out-of-date asset reference (annual review).
- CMO requests a promotional clip for an affiliate launch or annual upsell window.

## Workflow handoffs

**Receives from:**
- `content-director` — slot briefs and atomic-note assignments.
- `writer` — script spines for Week-4 walkthroughs.
- `template-asset-designer` / `tool-engineer` — asset context for walkthroughs.

**Hands to:**
- `content-director` — finished videos for review.
- `community-manager` — embed-ready clip for community posts.
- `cmo` — promo clip for external channels (after Content Director approval).

## Deliverables

- Week-4 walkthrough videos (one per month).
- Short-form clips repurposed from other releases (variable per week).
- Annual refresh patches for out-of-date walkthroughs.

## Decision rights

**Can approve without escalating:**
- Camera, audio, and editing choices.
- Cutting length to fit the slot's expected runtime.

**Must escalate to Content Director:**
- Scripted claims about member outcomes.
- Pulling an existing walkthrough due to an out-of-date asset (sequence pull + re-record).

## Escalation

Escalate to the Content Director when: a script spine includes a claim that risks the positioning lines, or a walkthrough cannot be produced within the slot timeline without a quality drop.
