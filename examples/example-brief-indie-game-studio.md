# Company Brief — Indie Game Studio (example)

A filled, sanitized example brief for the v0.1a quickstart. Copy
`examples/input-template.md` to start your own; this file shows what a complete,
valid brief looks like. Run `blueprints validate --input <this-file>` to confirm
it passes the offline rules (no API calls).

---

## 1. Company name and slug

**Name:** Indie Game Studio

**Slug:** indie-game-studio

**One-sentence description:** A solo-founder studio shipping one premium mobile puzzle game a year, sold once at a fair price.

---

## 2. North star

**Your north star:**

$30,000 monthly net revenue from premium sales, sustained for two consecutive quarters within 12 months of launch.

---

## 3. Goals

**Your goals:**

1. Maintain a 4.6+ App Store rating across the live title
2. A content update lands every 6 weeks on cadence, never slipping two cycles in a row
3. Refund rate stays below 3% quarter over quarter

---

## 4. We are

**Your "we are" paragraph:**

We are a single-title premium mobile studio. One game, polished relentlessly,
sold once at a fair price. We grow through word of mouth and store features, not
ad spend. Rating, refund rate, and update cadence are the only numbers that
matter on a given day.

---

## 5. We are NOT

**Your "we are not" list:**

1. **We are NOT** a free-to-play studio. We do not ship loot boxes, we do not run energy timers, and we do not gate content behind currency. Monetization is a single honest purchase, because the brand is "premium, no tricks."
2. **We are NOT** a multi-title shop. We do not split focus across a portfolio, and we do not start a second title while the live one needs work. The live title gets all attention, because focus is the only edge a solo studio has.

---

## 6. Constraints

**Your constraints:**

1. One title at a time. The live game gets every cycle until it is healthy.
2. No dark patterns. Every monetization decision is honest by default.
3. Store-facing changes (pricing, metadata, screenshots) wait for operator approval for the first 30 days.

---
