# Company Brief — cadence-fidelity probe fixture

Deliberately small: the probe runs identity + org planning only, so the cost is two model calls.
Section 7 states one cadence per grammar shape under test, plus one dependency.

## 1. Company name and slug

**Name:** Tideline Reports

**Slug:** tideline-reports

**One-sentence description:** A two-person research desk publishing a subscriber-only coastal shipping report.

## 2. North star

**Your north star:**

400 paying subscribers and EUR 6,000 monthly recurring revenue within 9 months of launch.

## 3. Goals

**Your goals:**

1. Subscriber churn below 4% per month, sustained across two quarters
2. Every published claim traceable to a dated primary source

## 4. We are

**Your "we are" paragraph:**

We are a small research desk. We publish slowly, source everything, and correct in public.

## 5. We are NOT

**Your "we are not" list:**

1. **We are NOT** a news wire. We do not chase breaking stories or same-day turnaround.
2. **We are NOT** a consultancy. We do not take client briefs or bespoke work.

## 6. Constraints

**Your constraints:**

1. Every claim carries a dated primary source. No exceptions.
2. One publication per week. Volume is never the answer.

## 7. Use case pattern (optional)

**Your choice:** custom

**Notes if customizing the pattern:**

Exactly three agents: an editor (the owner), a sources analyst reporting to the editor, and a
publication coordinator reporting to the editor. Do not add roles beyond these three.

Exactly three activities run on a standing schedule, and no others:

1. The sources analyst runs a **source sweep every week, on Tuesdays**.
2. The publication coordinator runs a **subscriber-list reconciliation monthly, on the 5th**.
3. The editor runs a **quarterly correction audit on the 8th of January, April, July and
   October**.

The subscriber-list reconciliation **consumes the output of the source sweep** — it works from
the entries the sweep has verified, and must run after it.

Everything else is handoff-driven, not scheduled.

## 8. Governance spectrum position

**Your choice:** balanced
